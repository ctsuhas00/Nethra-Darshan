The Stay

A place to slow down.

Nethra Darshan sits on the south hills above Mangalore, built into the slope so the Nethravathi River is visible from almost every room. Below the villa, wooded hills fold down toward the water; above it, a rooftop pool and sky bar look out over the valley.

The villa holds three bedrooms across three tiers, set on roughly an acre of fruit trees, seasonal vegetables and quiet trails — 15 km from Mangalore centre.
